MATHEMATICAL ENGINE

Inside the Engine: Scoring Formula

S_post = 0.40 × A_cat + 0.30 × E_res + 0.20 × E_pot + 0.10 × R
Term Definition & Implementation Description Range
$A_{\text{cat}}$ (40%) Category affinity normalised to user's highest-liked content category $[0, 1.0]$
$E_{\text{res}}$ (30%) Emotional tone resonance weight (outrage, fear, sensationalism) $[0, 1.0]$
$E_{\text{pot}}$ (20%) Engagement virality potential from post metadata divided by 10 $[0, 1.0]$
$R$ (10%) Recency boost: $\max(0.1, 1 - h/24)$ where $h$ = hours since publish $[0.1, 1.0]$
